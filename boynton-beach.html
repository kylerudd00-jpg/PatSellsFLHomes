Overview

The best value on the Palm Beach coastline.

Boynton Beach sits between Delray Beach to the south and Lake Worth to the north — close enough to both to share their amenities, but with a price point that makes it one of the most accessible coastal markets in Palm Beach County. The city has invested heavily in its downtown core, and the Boynton Beach Marina and Inlet area draws divers, boaters, and anglers year-round.

For buyers who want proximity to the water without the premium associated with Delray or Boca, Boynton Beach consistently delivers the most square footage per dollar in the area. Pat has helped multiple buyers identify off-market opportunities here before they hit the MLS.

Attractions

Water, wildlife, and a revived downtown.

Boynton Beach Inlet & Phil Foster Park

A beloved local spot for snorkeling the Phil Foster Park reef, watching sport fishing boats come in, and catching sunsets over the Intracoastal. Free public access.

Green Cay Wetlands

A 100-acre wetland preserve with an elevated boardwalk through marshes teeming with herons, anhingas, and alligators. One of the best birding spots in Palm Beach County.

Renaissance Commons

A walkable mixed-use development with shopping, dining, and a cinema — the anchor of Boynton's retail core and a hub for community events.

Oceanfront Park Beach

Boynton's main beach access — a well-maintained public beach with lifeguards, pavilions, and a playground. Less crowded than beaches in Delray or Boca.

Boynton Beach Marina

Home to dive charters, fishing trips, and waterfront restaurants. The reef diving off Boynton is among the best in Palm Beach County.

Barrier Free Park

An ADA-accessible park with adaptive playground equipment, paved trails, and a peaceful setting near the water. A thoughtful community asset.

Neighborhoods

Where buyers are finding value right now.

Hunters Run

A large gated golf community with multiple courses and amenities. Popular with active adults and buyers from the northeast looking for a full-service lifestyle.

Leisureville

One of Florida's original 55+ communities — affordable, well-maintained, and walkable. Strong resale market driven by consistent demand.

Ocean Ridge

A small, exclusive barrier island town within Boynton's zip code. Oceanfront and Intracoastal estates at significantly lower prices than equivalent properties in Manalapan or Palm Beach.

The Estates of Chapel Hill

Single-family homes on generous lots in western Boynton. Great schools, newer construction, and strong family appeal.

Dining & Lifestyle

Local spots worth knowing.

Two Georges Waterfront Grille

Dockside dining right on the Intracoastal — casual seafood, cold drinks, and boats passing by. A Boynton staple for over 30 years.

The Little House

A converted bungalow in downtown Boynton serving refined Florida cuisine. Small menu, fresh ingredients, genuine neighborhood energy.

Hurricane Alley Raw Bar

Laid-back waterfront bar with fresh oysters, fish tacos, and live music. The kind of place that keeps long-time Boynton residents loyal to the city.
