Selling Smart

What most sellers don't think about until it's too late.

Pricing is everything

Overpriced homes sit. Sitting homes get stigmatized. Pat's pricing analysis is built on real closed sales — not Zestimates or wishful thinking.

Timing the season

South Florida's peak buying season runs October through April. If you have flexibility, timing your list date can mean more competition among buyers and a higher final price.

What to fix (and what not to)

Not every improvement pays off at resale. Pat will walk through your home and tell you where a dollar spent returns two — and where it just costs you a dollar.

HOA and condo considerations

If you're in an HOA or condo association, buyers will scrutinize the financials. Pat helps you get ahead of anything that could slow down a contract.

Net proceeds vs. sale price

The sale price is a headline number. What matters is what you walk away with after commission, closing costs, taxes, and any required repairs. Pat runs the real numbers with you upfront.

Seller's disclosure

Florida law requires sellers to disclose known material defects. Pat guides you through what needs to be disclosed and how to handle it without killing deals.

Common Questions

What sellers ask Pat most.

How do you determine what my home is worth?

Pat does a Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) based on recently closed homes similar to yours in size, condition, and location. She'll walk you through the comps and explain why she landed on the range she did — no guessing, no flattery.

How long will it take to sell?

It depends on the price, condition, and time of year. A well-priced, well-prepared home in Delray Beach or Boca Raton typically goes under contract within a few weeks in season. Pat will give you a realistic timeline based on current market data when she reviews your property.

Do I need to be out of the house for showings?

Ideally yes — buyers are more candid and spend more time when sellers aren't present. Pat coordinates showings around your schedule and can handle lockbox access so you don't have to be there every time.

What if I'm also buying at the same time?

Coordinating a sale and purchase simultaneously is something Pat does regularly. She'll help you sequence the transactions, negotiate contingencies where needed, and keep both deals on track.

What does it actually cost to sell?

Seller's closing costs in Florida typically include real estate commission, title insurance, doc stamps on the deed, and any agreed-upon repairs or credits. Pat will give you a net proceeds estimate before you list so there are no surprises at closing.
