Overview

Space, schools, and a world-class equestrian scene.

Wellington is unlike anywhere else in Palm Beach County. About 20 minutes west of the coast, it's a master-planned village built around horses, family life, and an extraordinary school system. From January through April, it becomes an international destination — the Winter Equestrian Festival and the International Polo Club bring players and spectators from across the globe.

For buyers who don't need to be on the water, Wellington offers a level of space, quality, and amenity that simply doesn't exist at these prices anywhere coastal. Large lots, newer construction, and consistently strong resale demand make it one of the most stable markets in the county.

Attractions

Polo, parks, and an outdoor lifestyle.

International Polo Club Palm Beach

Sunday polo matches from January through April — free general admission, tailgating, and a social scene unlike anything else in Florida. World-class players, world-class setting.

Winter Equestrian Festival

One of the largest hunter/jumper competitions in the world, running 12 consecutive weeks at the Palm Beach International Equestrian Center. A cultural institution for the equestrian world.

Wellington Environmental Preserve

A 365-acre natural area with walking trails, fishing piers, and wetland habitat. Free public access and one of the best quiet spots in western Palm Beach County.

Wellington Amphitheater & Village Park

The community's outdoor event hub — concerts, food trucks, farmer's markets, and seasonal festivals throughout the year. A true neighborhood gathering place.

Neighborhoods

Where buyers are looking.

Palm Beach Polo & Country Club

Gated estates, polo fields, and golf — Wellington's most prestigious address. Buyers here want privacy, land, and proximity to the equestrian circuit.

Versailles

A popular gated community with a French-inspired aesthetic, pools, and well-maintained single-family homes. Strong demand from families relocating from the northeast.

Binks Forest

One of Wellington's most sought-after neighborhoods — large lots, mature trees, and access to top-rated Binks Forest Elementary.

Equestrian Club Estates

Direct access to bridle trails and the show grounds. Purpose-built for horse owners who want to ride from home to the circuit.

Dining & Lifestyle

Local favorites.

Tutto Fresco Trattoria

Reliable Italian in the heart of Wellington — wood-fired pizzas, fresh pasta, and a welcoming room. A local staple for family dinners and celebrations.

The Wanderer

A craft cocktail bar and kitchen with a thoughtful menu and a lively weekend crowd. One of Wellington's better spots for a night out.

Bluegill Kitchen & Bar

American comfort food done well — the kind of neighborhood spot where families eat on weeknights and the bar fills up on game days.
