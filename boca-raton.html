Overview

South Florida's most polished address.

Boca Raton has long been synonymous with upscale South Florida living — manicured streets, resort-style amenities, and a school district that consistently ranks among Florida's best. The city sits at the northern edge of Palm Beach County, bordered by the Atlantic to the east and a network of golf courses and nature preserves to the west.

Buyers here tend to know what they want: proximity to FAU or major employers, a specific school zone, waterfront access, or a particular gated community. Pat navigates these distinctions with the kind of familiarity that only comes from years working the local market.

Attractions

Culture, nature, and a resort-town pace.

Mizner Park

Boca's signature outdoor shopping and dining district — Mediterranean architecture, anchored by the Boca Raton Museum of Art and a concert amphitheater.

Boca Raton Museum of Art

A major regional museum with rotating exhibitions and a strong permanent collection of modern and contemporary works. Located inside Mizner Park.

Gumbo Limbo Nature Center

A beloved sea turtle rehabilitation center and coastal nature preserve. Educational programs, tank exhibits, and regular turtle release events.

Sugar Sand Park

146 acres of parkland with a science playground, carousel, tennis courts, and trails. A standout family destination in western Boca.

Boca Raton Resort & Club

The historic pink resort that defines Boca's identity — a Waldorf Astoria property with golf, beach club, spa, and world-class dining.

South Beach Park

One of the few fee-free beach accesses in Boca — quieter than public beaches further south, with soft sand and calm Atlantic surf.

Neighborhoods

Boca's most desirable enclaves.

Royal Palm Yacht & Country Club

Deep-water dockage, estate homes, and an exclusive country club. One of the most prestigious addresses in South Florida.

Broken Sound

A large gated community in west Boca with two championship golf courses, resort amenities, and a strong HOA. Popular with retirees and families alike.

Boca Pointe

Mid-range gated community near I-95 and FAU with a mix of condos and single-family homes. Great value for buyers who want the Boca address without the premium.

Via Verde

Quiet streets, oversized lots, and a central location close to excellent schools. No HOA in many sections — a draw for buyers who want flexibility.

Dining & Lifestyle

Where Boca eats and unwinds.

Kapow Noodle Bar

Asian-inspired noodles and small plates in a lively Mizner Park setting. A local favorite for lunch and happy hour.

Truluck's

Upscale seafood with a focus on fresh stone crab and sustainable catches. A reliable fine-dining choice for special occasions.

Bogart's Bar & Grille

A Boca institution — casual bar food, live music on weekends, and the kind of unpretentious atmosphere that balances out the city's reputation for polish.
