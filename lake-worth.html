Overview

Character and value, right on the coast.

Lake Worth Beach sits just north of Boynton Beach and south of West Palm — a compact city with a real downtown, a lively arts district, and a diverse mix of people that gives it more personality than most South Florida communities its size. It's been gentrifying steadily, and buyers who got in early have seen strong appreciation.

The housing stock is a mix of bungalows, older condos, and newer townhomes — most of it well-priced by coastal standards. Pat knows this market and has helped buyers find real value here before properties reach peak visibility.

Attractions

Beach, arts, and a downtown worth walking.

Lake Worth Beach

A free public beach with a casino building, pier, pool, and one of the most relaxed atmospheres on the Palm Beach coast. Rarely overcrowded.

Lake Worth Playhouse & Arts District

A walkable stretch of galleries, studios, and performance venues along Lake Avenue. Monthly art walks draw crowds from across the county.

Bryant Park

A beautiful waterfront park on Lake Worth Lagoon with a boat ramp, picnic areas, and views across to Palm Beach Island. A hidden gem.

Neighborhoods

Where buyers are finding value.

Downtown / Historic District

Bungalows and cottages within walking distance of Lake Avenue. High walkability, strong community identity, and prices still below comparable neighborhoods in Delray.

College Park

A quieter residential pocket west of downtown with good lot sizes and a mix of older single-family homes and recent renovations.

Suntree / Palm Beach Farms

Larger lots, newer construction, and a more suburban feel — with the convenience of being minutes from the downtown and the beach.

Dining & Lifestyle

Local spots worth knowing.

Bizzy's Bar and Grill

A downtown Lake Worth staple — casual, unpretentious, and reliably good. The kind of neighborhood restaurant that locals are protective of.

Oceanic Juice Bar & Café

Fresh juices, smoothie bowls, and light fare in the heart of the arts district. A go-to for the community's health-conscious crowd.

Brogues Downunder

An Australian-themed pub with an eclectic menu and a loyal local following. Live music on weekends and one of the better beer selections in the area.
