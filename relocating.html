Florida 101

Things no one tells you before you move.

No state income tax

Florida has no personal income tax. For buyers coming from high-tax states like New York, New Jersey, or Connecticut, this changes the real cost-of-living math significantly.

Homestead exemption

If you'll use the home as your primary residence, Florida's homestead exemption can save you thousands per year in property taxes. You apply after closing — Pat walks you through it.

Hurricane insurance

Homeowner's insurance in Florida is more expensive than most states — and wind/flood coverage adds to it. Pat makes sure you factor realistic insurance costs into your budget before you fall in love with a price point.

HOA culture

A large share of South Florida homes are in HOA communities. Rules, fees, and approval processes vary widely. Pat explains what you're getting into with each property before you make an offer.

Seasonal vs. year-round living

South Florida has a distinct season — it gets quieter (and cheaper to dine out) in summer. Some neighborhoods are mostly seasonal. Others are year-round. Pat can tell you what to expect from any specific community.

The right neighborhood for you

Delray Beach, Boca Raton, Boynton Beach, and Jupiter all feel different to actually live in. Pat gives you an honest comparison based on your lifestyle — not just price-per-square-foot.

Where to Look

Matching your lifestyle to the right community.

Delray Beach

Walkable, lively, coastal. Atlantic Ave is the social hub. A strong first choice for buyers who want to feel settled quickly.

Boca Raton

Top-rated schools, polished feel, strong northeast transplant community. Families from NY and NJ tend to land here.

Boynton Beach

More value for the money, coastal access, growing community. Good option if Delray or Boca prices are a stretch.

Jupiter

Relaxed pace, great schools, boating culture. Farther north but worth looking at if you prioritize outdoor lifestyle.

Wellington

Inland, spacious, equestrian country. Families who need space and low density at prices the coast can't match.
